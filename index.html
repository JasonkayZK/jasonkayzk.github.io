Research Interests

My journey into computing began with a background in Electrical Engineering, followed by several years of working as a software engineer. Over time, I became increasingly curious about what happens beneath the abstractions we use every day—how computers actually execute complex computations, and how we can make them faster and more efficient. That curiosity eventually led me toward AI Systems, Machine Learning Infrastructure, and High-Performance Computing.

These days, I'm exploring deep learning runtimes, compilers, GPU programming, and numerical computing. I'm particularly fascinated by the intersection of software and hardware, where clever system design can make a real difference in performance.

Looking ahead, I hope to pursue a Ph.D. in Computer Science, focusing on efficient AI systems and compiler–runtime co-design. I enjoy understanding things from first principles, building tools, and turning curiosity into something useful.
