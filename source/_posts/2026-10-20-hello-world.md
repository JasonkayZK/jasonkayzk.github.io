## Writing a new post

Create a file named `YYYY-MM-DD-your-title.md` in the `_posts` directory with
this front matter:

```yaml
title: "Your Title"
date: 2026-10-20 09:00:00 +08:00
tags: [systems, rust]
category: technical
excerpt: "One or two sentences shown on the blog index."
```
